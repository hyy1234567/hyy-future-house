# Example — Manuscript Review Under URS

## User request

> Strictly review this manuscript for journal submission.

## URS mode

`DEEP + CRITICAL REVIEW MODE`

## Review order

1. Research question and novelty
2. Study design and sampling
3. Data provenance and quality
4. Unit of analysis and statistical validity
5. Missingness, exclusions, multiplicity, and sensitivity analyses
6. Figure/Table validity and transparency
7. Claim Ceiling and overclaiming
8. Citation-to-claim support
9. Reproducibility and reporting-guideline compliance
10. Target-journal fit and current submission requirements

## Example finding

### Major issue

The manuscript uses causal language despite an observational design and no causal-identification strategy.

**Why it matters:** the study design supports association, not an unqualified causal claim.

**Required revision:** lower the Claim Ceiling from L4 causal to L1 association unless additional design or analysis supports stronger inference. Revise the title, abstract, Results interpretation, Discussion, and Conclusion consistently.

## Example final recommendation

`DECISION: MAJOR REVISION`

`CONFIDENCE: HIGH`

The decision is based on scientific and methodological validity rather than language polish alone.
