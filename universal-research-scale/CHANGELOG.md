# Changelog

All notable changes to Universal Research Scale (URS) will be documented here.

## v1.0 Final — 2026-09-06

Initial frozen core release.

### Added

- Evidence-first scientific workflow.
- Rolling five-year evidence policy with authoritative-guidance override.
- Task Diagnosis Engine.
- Evidence triangulation and saturation logic.
- Candidate comparison and Hard Gates.
- Figure/Table competition framework.
- Adaptive Publication Scale.
- Statistical Analysis & Inference Engine.
- Claim Ceiling and scientific writing controls.
- Citation-to-claim integrity rules.
- Reproducibility and provenance chain.
- Research-integrity safeguards.
- Critical Review Mode.
- Journal/submission decision rules.
- QUICK / STANDARD / DEEP modes.
- Cross-platform capability disclosure rules.
- Final multi-domain QC matrix.

### Version policy

v1.0 Final is the frozen core standard. Future development should add modular extensions such as URS-FIG, URS-STAT, URS-WRITE, URS-REVIEW, and URS-ML without weakening v1.0 Hard Gates.
